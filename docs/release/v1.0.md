## Release notes

### What's in 1.0

- **16 machines, one hall.** Restore a dead factory's machines one altar at a time, from the Intake Desk to the Last Rune. Each restored machine opens new doors. A full run takes about an hour.
- **Six gems:** Channel, Bend, Split, Amplify, Gate and Capacitor. Each altar gives a storm charge and a machine that needs an exact amount. Place gems to route and shape the charge, then Strike. Too much trips the breaker. Too little and nothing wakes.
- **Clear feedback.** Gives and Needs labels, a live route preview, charge chips on the bolt, and a verdict after every strike that says what went wrong and what to try next.
- **FUSE,** the facility's maintenance unit, who has opinions about your safety record. He talks you through the first altars, comments on your strikes and has a story of his own.
- **The journal** (J): 16 pages of the Builder's notes, found as machines wake. **The codex** (C): a page for every gem you meet, with diagrams of how charge goes in and out.
- **An ending** with a short epilogue and an end card. On a replay you can skip straight to the card.
- **Settings** (O): reduced motion, reduce flashes, caption speed, text size 100/125/150%, graphics Auto/High/Low, and a progress reset.
- **Play your way.** Mouse, keyboard only, gamepad, or touch on iPad. Screen-reader announcements for the altar briefing and verdicts.
- **Saves automatically** in your browser's local storage. Nothing is sent anywhere.
- **All art and sound are procedural**, drawn and synthesised in the browser on the ThunderClap engine (WebGPU). The whole game is a single script, under 300 KB zipped.

### Known limits

- **WebGPU is required.** Chrome or Edge 113+ on Windows, macOS and ChromeOS; Safari 26+ on macOS and iPadOS. Without WebGPU the game shows a page explaining what to use instead.
- **Phones come in 1.1.** Phones are not supported in 1.0. They get a proper layout in Last Rune 1.1, built on the engine's new layout model. iPad in landscape is supported.
- **Firefox is not a target.** Recent Firefox builds with WebGPU may run the game, but it is not tested.
- **Linux** Chrome needs WebGPU turned on by hand.
- **Saves stay in one browser.** Clearing site data deletes your progress, and there is no cloud sync.
- **Colour emoji** in the ending's chat need a system emoji font. Without one, they appear as plain symbols.

## Devlog: The Last Rune 1.0 is out

> **The Last Rune 1.0 is out**
>
> The Last Rune is a short puzzle game about routing lightning through gems to wake the machines of an abandoned factory. It's free to play in your browser, on desktop or iPad.
>
> Everything you see and hear is made by code at runtime. There are no image or audio files in the download, just one script, under 300 KB zipped. It runs on ThunderClap, a small WebGPU engine I built alongside it. The Last Rune is the engine's first game.
>
> A lot of 1.0 went into making the puzzles readable without a manual. Each altar shows what the storm gives and what the machine needs. The route lights up as you build it. After a failed strike, the game tells you what went wrong and suggests a next step. FUSE helps too, in his own way.
>
> **What's next.** Phones aren't supported in 1.0. Small screens need a different layout, not just a smaller one, so 1.1 brings phone support built on a new layout model in the engine. Until then, please play on a desktop browser or an iPad in landscape.
>
> **Requirements.** You need a browser with WebGPU: Chrome or Edge 113+, or Safari 26+. Firefox isn't a target for now.
>
> If you finish it, the end card has a link to the ThunderClap Gaming Discord, where FUSE ended up. Thanks for playing.
